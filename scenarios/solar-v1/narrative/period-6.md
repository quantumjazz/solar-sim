# Period 6 — Tax hike, one-off remediation, market 2 weakens

A modest recovery is under way — real GDP is forecast to grow about 1.4 % —
but it is a cost-pushed one: inflation runs near 4 % and wages rise 3 %.
To close a budget gap, the government raises the tax on profits to 55 %.

## News from the industry

Customers are paying more slowly. The share of a period's sales collected
within that same period falls to about 70 %, with the remainder carried
into the following period as receivables. This squeezes cash even for
profitable firms.

The export market weakens further as the foreign currency depreciates,
eroding the home-currency value of market-2 sales.

**A one-off charge lands.** Environmental inspectors have ordered the
mandatory repair of an industrial-waste purification plant. The work costs
6.0 mEUR and is booked as a one-off expense this period — plan the cash
position around it.

A bulk buyer will take up to 7,000 Gen-1 kits at 2,700 EUR. The Gen-1
panel segment is now small; most panel demand has moved to Gen-2.
