# Period 2 — Inflation lifts; the Central Bank cuts rates; suppliers raise prices

The economy is stabilising. To encourage investment, the Central Bank is
lowering its key interest rate. Economists forecast real GDP growth of
about 2 %, a 2 % rise in the cost of living, and broadly flat wages. Good
staff remain hard to find despite the slack labour market, and the average
cost of a new hire rises.

## News from the industry

Demand for residential PV kits is firming — the domestic market is
expected to grow by roughly 9 %.

Component suppliers have announced a general price increase of about 3 %
for input materials.

A public-sector buyer has issued a **tender** for 3,000 system kits, to be
delivered next period; the lowest valid bid wins. A returning bulk buyer
will take up to 8,000 kits per company at 2,450 EUR.

**A successor product can now be developed.** Marketing studies point to
demand for a higher-efficiency residential kit. From this period, companies
may begin research and development on a Gen-2 system. It cannot be launched
before period 4, and its current prototype indices are well below the
Gen-1 product — sustained R&D will be needed to make it competitive.

An equipment maker is offering its Full-Auto Module Line at a one-period
promotional price of 22.5 mEUR (catalogue price 30 mEUR).
