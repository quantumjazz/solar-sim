# Period 8 — Storage and balcony solar accelerate; panels cool

Growth is steady — about 1.5 % in real terms — with low inflation and
wages up 2 %. The profit tax returns to its long-run level of 30 %, easing
the burden on earnings.

## News from the industry

The product mix is rotating. Battery storage and balcony solar are now the
fastest-growing segments, while panel demand — though still the largest
single market — is cooling as the replacement wave matures. The Gen-1 kit
is all but retired; a manufacturer still leaning on it has very little
market left to serve.

A team's standing now depends heavily on how well it read the earlier
signals: whether it launched Gen-2 in good time, whether it entered storage
and balcony solar, and whether its production fleet meets the tighter
environmental baseline.
