# Period 5 — Tighter environmental rules; balcony-solar segment opens

The downturn has reached its trough. Output is roughly flat — a forecast
real GDP change of about −0.3 % — wages are frozen, and the cost of living
rises 1.2 %. Banks are tightening: the overdraft rate moves up again.

## News from the industry

**A stricter environmental law takes effect.** Under the Environmental Act
KWA II §4, production lines are reclassified downward — every existing
line loses 15 environmental-index points — and the compliance baseline
rises from 100 to 115. A manufacturer whose fleet sits below the baseline
pays a penalty for every missing point. Newer lines are cleaner; modernising
the fleet is the route back into compliance.

Input materials for the ageing Gen-1 kit jump about 15 % as order volumes
fall and the supplier loses scale. Trucking taxes push distribution costs
sharply higher in both markets. A union agreement ends overtime for now and
raises the standard pension contribution.

**Two new segments open.** Battery storage and a plug-in balcony-solar kit
become launchable this period. Storage is a separate, capital-intensive
product; balcony solar is a higher-volume, lower-margin mid-market kit that
can also be sourced from a contract manufacturer.
