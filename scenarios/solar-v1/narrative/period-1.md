# Period 1 — Modest recovery; first bulk-buyer offer appears

After the capital-market turmoil of recent years, economists expect a
cautious recovery over the coming year. Gross domestic product is forecast
to grow by about 2 % in real terms, the cost of living to stay roughly
flat, and wages and salaries to rise by around 3 %. Banks remain reluctant
lenders, so a number of firms are still holding back on investment.

## News from the industry

Demand for residential PV system kits is recovering. Industry analysts
expect the domestic market to grow by roughly 7 % over the year, with
replacement installations driving most of the increase.

Polysilicon and component prices have stabilised after a volatile spell.
The association of module assemblers expects assembly-consumable costs to
ease slightly, to about 46 EUR per unit.

A regional retail chain has approached manufacturers with a bulk-purchase
offer: up to 5,000 system kits per company at a fixed 2,500 EUR per unit.
Bulk orders carry no distribution cost and take precedence over retail
supply, but the margin is thinner than a retail sale.
