# Period 3 — Export market opens; Gen-2 launchable next period

The economy is performing well, with strong demand at home and abroad.
Economists forecast real GDP growth of about 3 % and a 2 % rise in the cost
of living. In recent collective bargaining, employees accepted a freeze on
real wages in exchange for job security: no production staff may be
dismissed this period, and the working week is shorter.

## News from the industry

**A second market opens.** An association of installers has drawn attention
to a foreign market — "market 2" — for residential PV. It is small at first,
roughly a tenth of the domestic market, but is expected to grow quickly.
Market 2 trades in a foreign currency; the home market keeps delivery
priority over it. Selling there requires sales staff allocated to it.

A bulk buyer will take up to 9,000 kits at 2,475 EUR. A leasing company has
tendered for 14,000 kits, for delivery next period.

Input-material prices rise a further 3 %. The shorter working week reduces
how many units one production worker can complete in a period.

**Battery storage on the horizon.** Market research indicates growing
demand for residential battery-storage systems. From this period, companies
may begin developing a storage product; it cannot be launched before
period 5.

A reminder: the Gen-2 system kit becomes launchable next period. A team
that has not developed its indices by then will launch a weak product.
