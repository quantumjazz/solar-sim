# Period 9 — Final period: settle, then score

The economy is steady — real GDP growth of about 1.2 %, low inflation,
wages up 1.5 %. There are no policy surprises in the scoring period.

## News from the industry

The market has finished its transition. Gen-2 panels, battery storage, and
balcony solar are the three live segments; the Gen-1 kit is effectively
retired. Demand in every continuing segment grows once more.

This is the final period. The company is valued as it stands at the close —
there is no "next year" to plan for. Decisions that only pay back beyond
period 9 — a production line bought now, a deep R&D push — will cost cash
and earnings this period without a return, so weigh end-game moves against
their effect on the closing balance sheet.

Owners' equity at the end of this period is the figure that determines the
result. Settle inventory, collect what can be collected, avoid an
unnecessary loss, and finish clean.
