# Period 7 — Trade duties cut; storage demand surges

Conditions are improving. Real GDP growth of about 1 % is expected, with
the cost of living almost flat and wages up 1.5 %. The government rolls
the profit tax back to 45 %, and customer payment behaviour recovers a
little.

## News from the industry

A round of trade negotiations has concluded, and import duties on the
export market fall — goods move more freely, and contract-manufactured
balcony-solar kits become slightly cheaper to source.

Demand for residential battery storage is climbing quickly as households
pair storage with their panels. The balcony-solar segment continues to
grow. Gen-2 panels are now the clear mainstream product; the Gen-1 segment
is residual.

A new bulk buyer offers 2,800 EUR for up to 7,000 kits and, having been
burned elsewhere, pays in advance — a useful cash inflow.
