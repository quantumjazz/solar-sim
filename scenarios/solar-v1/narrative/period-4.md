# Period 4 — Recession in market 1; Gen-2 can launch

The first scored period opens against a weakening economy. Fears of
overheating have prompted the Central Bank to restore interest rates to
their earlier, higher level, and government spending has been trimmed.
Economic institutes now forecast a real GDP contraction of about 1 %, with
the cost of living up 3 % and wages up 3 %.

## News from the industry

Domestic demand for residential PV cools as households defer big-ticket
spending; analysts expect the market-1 panel segment to shrink. Export
demand, by contrast, holds up — market 2 grows by roughly 20 %.

**The Gen-2 system kit is launchable this period.** A team whose Gen-2
efficiency and sustainability indices have reached commercially viable
levels (around 100 each) can put it on sale; launching a weak product
risks a sharp loss of customer confidence. Gen-1 and Gen-2 compete for the
same households — over the coming periods the panel market shifts decisively
toward the new generation.

A foreign government has tendered for 9,000 kits at a maximum acceptable
price of 2,800 EUR, for delivery next period. A bulk buyer will take up to
7,000 kits at 2,650 EUR.
